# read-me

Status: **DEFAULT on vague / slangy / contradictory asks**

## Job

At most three questions. Restate the job in plain English. Wait for a nod. Do not invent product. Then hand to feature-plan or ui-thrift.

## Ordinary English is the spec

They will not name skills. They may not know the word for the bug.

Load `references/plain-en.md` for slangy / incomplete / British-informal asks if that file exists.

Never instruct them to say fail codes or slash commands. Decode, ask at most three questions, restate, wait.

Screenshots outrank vocabulary.

## Decode (ordinary English)

| They say | You hear |
| --- | --- |
| Doesn’t work on my phone | Primary path fails on the device they use |
| Keyboard’s on the words | Inset / caret covered — do not restyle |
| That bottom thing / type menu | The sheet in the shot |
| Doing that thing again | Last bug + last screenshots |
| Looks decent but not like an app | Behaviour and chrome, not a new face |
| Polish the iOS bits | just-works bar leftovers |
| Don’t go mad | No new look, stack, or extra screens |

You talk. I decode. Slash commands are optional backup.
